# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to translate backlog items and acceptance criteria into implementation.
- Coordinate with Project Managers on delivery milestones, dependencies, and risks.
- Partner with QA/Testing Lead to validate quality before release.

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Align with Project Managers on timelines, dependencies, and milestone readiness.
- Provide direction and priority to Developers and Technical Leads during planning and delivery.
- Partner with Stakeholders/Sponsors on business outcomes, funding, and strategic trade-offs.

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work closely with Product Managers to track scope, schedule, and stakeholder expectations.
- Coordinate work across Developers, Technical Leads, and QA/Testing Lead to manage dependencies and delivery flow.
- Escalate risks and blockers to Stakeholders/Sponsors when business or cross-functional decisions are required.

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA / Testing Leads ensure product quality, validate acceptance criteria, and coordinate testing strategy across the project lifecycle. They work with engineering and product teams to catch defects early and confirm release readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and plans for features, fixes, and integrations
- Review acceptance criteria and identify quality risks early
- Coordinate manual and automated testing activities
- Track defects, regressions, and quality trends
- Validate that release readiness criteria are met before deployment

### Goals
- Catch issues before they reach customers
- Improve confidence in feature quality and reliability
- Reduce the cost of rework and production incidents

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and sign-off meetings
- Defect triage and release readiness reviews
- QA dashboards and release summaries

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Developers to review testability, coverage, and defect fixes.
- Support Product Managers by validating whether features meet stated acceptance criteria.
- Coordinate with Project Managers on QA timelines, dependencies, and release gating.
- Communicate quality risks and blockers with Stakeholders/Sponsors when issues affect milestone or business outcomes.

---

## Stakeholders / Sponsors

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business direction, funding, and decisions that influence project priorities and outcomes. They help define the strategic context for the work and approve major decisions, trade-offs, and escalations.

### Responsibilities
- Define business priorities, investment decisions, and expected outcomes
- Review status against milestones and success metrics
- Provide approvals for scope, resources, and key decisions
- Support escalation of risk, dependency, or budget issues

### Goals
- Maximize business value and strategic alignment
- Maintain confidence in project direction and delivery
- Ensure timely decision-making and accountability

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and steering committee updates
- Weekly or monthly status check-ins
- Decision and escalation meetings

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to confirm priorities and success metrics.
- Align with Project Managers on schedule, dependencies, and escalation paths.
- Support Developers and Technical Leads by clarifying business constraints, trade-offs, and strategic goals.
- Receive status and risk updates from QA and delivery leads before final go/no-go decisions.

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical direction, maintain architectural integrity, and help the team reduce technical debt and delivery risk. They ensure solutions are scalable, reliable, and aligned with engineering standards.

### Responsibilities
- Review architecture, design, and technical trade-offs
- Guide implementation patterns and integration decisions
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and mitigation options
- Mentor developers and support technical decision-making
- Help maintain maintainability, security, and operational readiness

### Goals
- Deliver quality technical solutions that scale over time
- Reduce delivery risk and rework
- Maintain alignment between product needs and engineering constraints

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and architecture decision sessions
- Technical planning discussions and risk review meetings
- Guidance for implementation standards and operational concerns

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to assess feasibility, sequencing, and technical trade-offs.
- Support Developers by clarifying implementation patterns and design constraints.
- Provide technical risk input to Project Managers for milestone planning and dependency management.
- Collaborate with QA/Testing Lead on testability, production readiness, and integration validation.

---

## Security Officer / Security Lead

### Role Summary
Security Officers or Security Leads protect the organization by ensuring security requirements are designed into the solution and by supporting incident response when needed. They help project teams address risk before and during release.

### Responsibilities
- Define security requirements, controls, and review expectations
- Review new functionality for risk, compliance, and vulnerability exposure
- Coordinate security scans, assessments, and remediation activities
- Support incident response and security-related escalations
- Maintain security documentation and release readiness standards

### Goals
- Reduce security risk and vulnerability exposure
- Improve confidence in secure delivery practices
- Support compliant, resilient project execution

### Typical Communication
- Security requirements workshops and design reviews
- Release security sign-off and incident response coordination
- Security findings and remediation updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Developers and Technical Leads to review security implications of implementation choices.
- Align with Project Managers on risk timing, release gates, and remediation planning.
- Support Product Managers and Stakeholders/Sponsors with risk trade-offs and compliance impact.
- Partner with QA/Testing Lead to include security validations in test and release workflows.

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach (Optional)

### Role Summary
A Scrum Master or Agile Coach helps the team stay focused on delivery by facilitating agile practices, improving team collaboration, and supporting continuous improvement. This role is optional and can be added where a team uses Scrum or agile delivery patterns extensively.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, and retrospectives
- Help remove blockers and improve delivery flow
- Support team practices, rituals, and process improvement
- Promote healthy collaboration and clear ownership of work

### Goals
- Improve team effectiveness and predictability
- Reduce friction in the delivery process
- Support a learning-oriented, high-performing team culture

### Typical Communication
- Standups, retrospectives, and sprint reviews
- Team process improvement discussions
- Dependency and blocker follow-up with project leadership

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Support Product Managers and Project Managers by improving planning and feedback loops.
- Helps Developers maintain visible progress and reduce ambiguity in execution.
- Collaborates with QA/Testing Lead to keep quality checks integrated into the sprint flow.
- Provides escalation support for process barriers that might otherwise delay delivery.
